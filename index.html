👗 Dress Code, Couples & Friday Rhythm

• Modern Cities & Stadiums: Casual Western attire is completely normal in Casablanca, Rabat, Tangier, Agadir & fan zones.

• Historic Medinas: Covering shoulders and knees shows respect and prevents unwanted attention.

• Foreign Unmarried Couples: Can freely book hotels and Riads together upon presenting valid passports.

🏜️ Trip 1: Marrakech ➔ Agafay & Essaouira (72h)

Day 1: Sunset camel ride & Berber dinner in Agafay Stone Desert (40 min from Marrakech).

Day 2–3: CTM/Supratours bus to Essaouira (2h30) for Atlantic seafood & historic ramparts.

🔵 Trip 2: Tangier ➔ Chefchaouen & Asilah (72h)

Day 1–2: Bus or Grand Taxi to Chefchaouen (2h) to explore the famous Blue Pearl Medina.

Day 3: Stop at Asilah (45 min south) for whitewashed art walls and grilled fish.

🏄 Trip 3: Agadir ➔ Taghazout & Paradise Valley

25 min north of Agadir: world-class surf villages (Taghazout & Imsouane) and natural palm pools.

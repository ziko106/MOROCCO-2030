🥘 Iconic Moroccan Gastronomy

• Royal Friday Couscous: Steamed semolina with 7 vegetables and tender meat every Friday.

• Pastilla: Crispy pastry filled with spiced chicken or seafood, almonds, and cinnamon.

• Rfissa & Tanjia: Shredded msemen with lentils (Rfissa) or slow-cooked clay-pot beef (Tanjia).

• Mint Tea: Poured from high above—always accept with your right hand.

🕌 Traditions & Etiquette

• Hospitality: Say "Salam Alaykum" with a hand over your heart.

• Mosques: Non-Muslims can visit the stunning Hassan II Mosque in Casablanca via guided tours.

• Souk Bargaining: Friendly ritual—smile and start around 50% of the first quote.

🏜️ The Moroccan Sahara: Heritage & Global Recognition

Morocco's Southern Provinces (Laâyoune and Dakhla) are an integral part of the Kingdom's history and identity.

🌍 International Backing: Following the December 2020 US Proclamation recognizing Moroccan sovereignty, over 30 nations—including Spain, France, and Arab & African allies—back Morocco's Autonomy Plan with consulates in Laâyoune and Dakhla.

🌊 Southern Jewels & 2030 Mega-Projects

• Dakhla Lagoon: World kitesurfing capital where Sahara dunes meet turquoise Atlantic waters.

• Dakhla Atlantic Port & Tiznit-Dakhla Expressway: $1.2B mega-port and 1,055 km highway linking the Southern Provinces.

✈️ Flight Tip: Daily 2h flights from Casablanca (CMN) to Dakhla (VIL) & Laâyoune (EUN).

👗 Dress Code, Couples & Friday Rhythm

• Modern Cities & Stadiums: Casual Western attire is completely normal in Casablanca, Rabat, Tangier, Agadir & fan zones.

• Historic Medinas: Covering shoulders and knees shows respect and prevents unwanted attention.

• Friday Prayer (Jumu'ah): Many small medina shops close 12:30–14:30 on Fridays—ideal time for Royal Friday Couscous.

• Foreign Unmarried Couples: Can freely book hotels and Riads together upon presenting valid passports.

📸 Photography & Drone Customs Laws

• Drones Strictly Banned: Unlicensed drones are immediately confiscated at Moroccan airports without prior government permit.

• Restricted Buildings: Never photograph police officers, military personnel, border posts or Royal Palaces.

• Souks & Street Performers: Always ask permission before portraits; Jemaa el-Fna performers expect 10–20 MAD.
